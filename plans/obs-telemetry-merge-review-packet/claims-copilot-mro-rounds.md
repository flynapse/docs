  implementer's mutation proof never earns tier 0 here. That yields **17 tier-0 rows**, from three

- **Tier 2** where the row decides what a signal carries (names, units, attributes, labels), what content
  crosses into telemetry, storage or the DOM, tenancy, or a board's meaning.
- **Tier 1** for everything else: defect fixes and guard fixes under an already-settled rule, merge
  resolutions, process.
- **Severity** is the reviewers' own scale, moved out of their "Tier" column: 0 = a content leak that
  ships, 1 = guard or lock integrity, 2 = a coverage gap, 3 = docs or process. Rounds that used P1/P2 or
  HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW keep that label beside the mapped number.
- **`State 2026-09-22`** separates **FIXED, independently re-verified** (a reviewer saw it) from **FIXED /
