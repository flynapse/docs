### How Tier, Severity and State were assigned

- **Tier** (§2.3a) was assigned here:
  - **Tier 0** only where an **independent reviewer saw the guard fail** on the defeating input, through its own
    mutation, its own plant, or a re-run after the fix, and the row carries no open judgment. That independent
    reviewer can be this round's or a later round's. An implementer's mutation proof never earns tier 0.
  - **Tier 2** where the row decides what a signal carries (the server span's attribute split), what a
    notification payload carries, or a complete-read contract over the tenant registry that rests on another
    repo's reader.
  - **Tier 1** for everything else: the checkout pin's mechanics, Rule B's detector shapes, prose, process.
- **Severity** is the reviewers' scale: 0 = a content leak that ships; 1 = guard or lock integrity, including a
  session that silently tests the wrong tree; 2 = a coverage gap; 3 = docs, process or cosmetic. The reviewer's own
  label stays beside the number (P1/P2/P3, or the lens reviewers' "TIER n", which is a severity scale and has been
  moved here). `—` means nothing is owed.
- **Chunk:** F1 = contract and privacy (what spans, logs and payloads carry; the guards protecting them); F2 = merge
  integrity (the checkout pin, Rule B, the walk, registers, carries); F3 = residual, docs, deferred.
- **`State 2026-09-22`** opens with one of these:
  - `FIXED-AT <sha>`, followed by who proved it: `(independent …)` a reviewer other than the implementer saw it red;
    `(implementer)` only the implementer's own proof exists.
  - `FIXED (carried)`: a carry-spec item that landed in the sibling trees, measured by md5 here.
  - `FIXED (plan)`: a document correction.
  - `HOLDS`: a reviewer-verified claim still true at `e3ba207`.
  - `SEE claims-api-r6/r8 row X`: the current state was settled there.
  - `SUPERSEDED-BY <commit or ruling>`: the decision was replaced, not patched.
  - `PARTIAL`, `STILL OPEN`, `ACCEPTED` (a declared limit or a design choice), `ROUTED` (another repo owns it),
    `REFUTED-BY` (a later finding contradicted the claim).
