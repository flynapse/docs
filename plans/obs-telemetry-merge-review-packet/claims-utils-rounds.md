### How Tier, Severity and State were assigned

- **Tier** (§2.3a) was assigned here:
  - **Tier 0** only where an **independent reviewer saw the guard fail** on the defeating input, through its own mutation, its own plant, or a re-run of the plant after the fix, and the row carries no open judgment. That independent reviewer can be this round's, a later round's, or the commissioned G.10 reviewer's. An implementer's mutation proof never earns tier 0.
  - **Tier 2** where the row decides what a signal carries (names, attributes, outcome vocabulary, a board's meaning), what content reaches a log, span or stdout under a new policy, tenancy, or an irreversible action.
  - **Tier 1** for everything else: defect fixes and guard fixes under a rule already settled, pinning mechanics, process.
- **Severity** is the reviewers' scale:
  - 0 = a content leak that ships;
  - 1 = guard or lock integrity, including a false `success` or a signal that operators rely on;
  - 2 = a coverage gap;
  - 3 = docs, process or cosmetic.

  The reviewer's own label stays beside the number: P1/P2, HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW, or the lens reviewers' "TIER n", which is a severity scale and has been moved here. `—` means nothing is owed.
- **Chunk:** F1 = contract and privacy (withholding, signals, the guards that protect them); F2 = merge integrity (checkout pin, G.92 seat, registers, commit hygiene); F3 = residual, docs, deferred.
- **`State 2026-09-22`** opens with one of these:
  - `FIXED-AT <sha>`, followed by who proved it:
    - `(independent …)`: a reviewer other than the implementer saw it;
    - `(implementer)`: only the implementer's own proof exists.
  - `HOLDS`: a reviewer-verified claim that is still true at `849ca21`.
  - `SEE claims-utils-r5/r6 row X`: the current state was settled there.
  - `PARTIAL`
  - `STILL OPEN`
  - `SUPERSEDED-BY <ruling or redesign>`
  - `ACCEPTED` / `ACCEPTED-AS-DEBT`
  - `DEFERRED (§6)`
  - `OWNER-OWED`
  - `REFUTED-BY`: a later independent review contradicted the finding.
  - `ROUTED`: another repo owns it; that slice holds the proof.
