**What this chunk is.** Every decision in the `obs-telemetry-merge` plan that no test settles by itself:
the six Phase 0 standalone-review lanes' ACCEPT-WITH-FIX findings whose fix was a *judgment* call, the
deferred register (plan §6), and every owner ruling (§4a), every "call made here, owner may overturn"
(§4b, §4b-corrections), every rulings-after-Phase-0 (§4d-resolved) and every P0 disposition (§4e).

**What an auditor should do with it.** Adjudicate the *claims*, not the code. Each row states the decision,
why it was taken, what corroborates it in the tree today, and what guards it. Rows marked `OPEN` rest on
judgment with nothing pinning them — those are the rows worth spending tokens on, tier 2 first. Rows marked
`SETTLED` carry a named guard AND a mutation the plan or ledger records as having failed it; spot-check only
if you disbelieve the mutation. `ASSERTED` rows have a guard but no recorded mutation — the cheapest useful
check is to break the property and see whether the named test notices.
