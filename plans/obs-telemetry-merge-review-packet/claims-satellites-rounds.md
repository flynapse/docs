## Scales

- **Severity** is the reviewer's own number, moved out of the column they called "Tier" where that column meant
  severity (G.110 and TG-R2 used 0 = a leak / 1 / 2 and "T0…T3"; SO-R0 used "T0/T1/T2"). Where a round used P-labels,
  REAL-GAP/QUESTIONABLE, HIGH/MED, or a "Tier" on another axis (IR's 0 = closed in flight / 1 = defect / 2 =
  judgment), the Severity is derived and marked `*`:
  - `0*` a content, secret or personal-data leak that ships;
  - `1*` a correctness or availability defect, a false success or a wrong signal operators rely on, or a gate, lock
    or guard-integrity defect (a guard that crashes, over-fires, or loses sync and blinds a whole file);
  - `2*` a coverage gap (a blind guard or an unguarded property, with no live instance);
  - `3*` docs, prose or process.

  `—` means the claim holds and there is no defect.
- **Tier** is §2.3a's scale, assigned by the assembler:
  - **0** — a verifiable property that an **independent reviewer** saw a named guard fail on under a named mutation
    or plant, with nothing later refuting it. A reviewer re-running its own plant after the fix counts. **A reviewer
    commissioned by the implementer it reviewed (IP, IR, IS) never earns tier 0**, as N5 applied to its FO-R0: its
    report reached the implementer, not the controller (CHECKPOINT 10c), and two of the three reviewed a tree that
    moved under them. **An implementer's own mutation proof never buys tier 0**, and a measured lane count is not a
    guard seen failing (the sub-assembler's tier 0 on TG1-21, TG2-17 and SO0-18 is corrected to 1 here).
  - **1** — consequential but reversible.
  - **2** — irreversible or estate-shaping: what an exported span, log or API body CARRIES (the withholding rules are
    a wire contract); an alarm's or a board's meaning and the dialect it is written in; tenancy teardown and personal
    data; a wire contract another repo consumes; deploy and publish order; an owner ruling.

  A tier-2 decision stays tier 2 even when its code is settled: the guard proves the implementation, not the judgment.
- **Chunk.** Tier 2 goes to F1. Tier 0/1 rows whose subject is what leaves the process (exception text, chat text,
  secrets, content flags, PII), tenancy, or an alarm's truth go to F1. Guard mechanics, pins, registers, CI wiring,
  commit hygiene and tracing coverage go to F2. Deferred, owner-owed, accepted debt, prose, process and other-repo
  pointers go to F3.
- **Claim state (at review)**, as that reviewer found it: SETTLED, ASSERTED, OPEN, REFUTED or PARTIAL (a reviewer's own
  word — VERIFIED, CLOSED, CLOSED IN FLIGHT — is kept beside the mapped state).
- **State 2026-09-22**, verified at the HEADs above. It opens with one of: `HOLDS` · `FIXED-AT <sha> ·
  reviewer-proved (<who>)` / `· reviewer-held` (a later reviewer read or drove it without a mutation) /
  `· controller-verified` / `· implementer-proved only` · `FIXED (prose/plan/process)` · `STILL OPEN` ·
  `SUPERSEDED-BY <M-* or a later commit>` · `ACCEPTED-AS-DEBT` · `DEFERRED` · `OWNER-OWED` · `REFUTED` (the
  reviewer's claim was refuted — by whom) · `SEE <file> row <id>` (a later filed round settled it there) ·
  `pending <batch>` (a live implementer has it; nothing committed).
