## Scales

- **Severity** is the reviewer's own number, moved out of the column they called "Tier" where that column meant
  severity (G.110: 0 = leak / 1 / 2; telegram-bot R1 and R2: 0/1/2 and "T0…T3"; shift-optimizer R0: "T0/T1/T2";
  iac-Ra: 0 = closed-in-flight / 1 = defect / 2 = judgment — a different axis, so every iac-Ra severity is derived).
  `*` means the assembler derived it:
  - `0*` a content, secret or personal-data leak that ships;
  - `1*` a correctness or availability defect, a false success or a wrong signal operators rely on, or a gate, lock
    or guard-integrity defect;
  - `2*` a coverage gap (a blind guard or an unguarded property, with no live instance);
  - `3*` docs, prose or process.

  `—` means the claim holds and there is no defect.
- **Tier** is §2.3a's scale, assigned by the assembler:
  - **0** — a verifiable property that an **independent reviewer** saw a named guard fail on under a named
    mutation or plant, with nothing later refuting it. A reviewer re-running its own plant after the fix counts.
    An implementer's own mutation proof never buys tier 0. It never goes to Fable.
  - **1** — consequential but reversible.
  - **2** — irreversible or estate-shaping: what an exported span, log or API body CARRIES; an alarm's or a
    board's meaning and the dialect it is written in; tenancy teardown and personal data; a wire contract another
    repo consumes; deploy order; an owner ruling.

  A tier-2 decision stays tier 2 even when its code is settled.
- **Chunk.** Tier 2 goes to F1. Tier 0/1 rows whose subject is what leaves the process (exception text, chat text,
  secrets, PII), tenancy, or an alarm's truth go to F1. Guard mechanics, pins, registers, CI wiring, commit hygiene
  and tracing coverage go to F2. Deferred, owner-owed, accepted debt, prose, process and other-repo pointers go
  to F3.
- **Claim state (at review)**, as that reviewer found it: SETTLED, ASSERTED, OPEN, REFUTED or PARTIAL (the iac-Ra
  table's own words — VERIFIED, CLOSED-IN-FLIGHT — are kept beside the mapped state).
